A breathtaking aerial drone photograph of Istanbul at golden hour, seen from high above the historic peninsula. The Hagia Sophia and the Blue Mosque with its six slender minarets stand in the foreground among red-tiled rooftops, Topkapi Palace and green gardens at the tip of the peninsula. The Bosphorus strait winds through the city with ferries leaving white wakes on the blue water, the Galata Tower and the dense hills of Beyoglu on the other side of the Golden Horn, and the Bosphorus Bridge in the hazy distance connecting Europe and Asia. Warm low sunlight, soft long shadows, seagulls in the air. Wide angle, high altitude, sharp focus, natural colors, realistic travel photography.
